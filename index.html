A manifestation draws from your reserve. Longer reach and greater strength demand more of the Source. Reeling consumes additional reserve. Rest in meditation outside combat to recover. Meditation and editing lock during combat. Hooking an opponent pulls them toward you automatically. Outside combat, tap Reel to start or stop. Four sword attacks finish with a heavy strike.

Touch: left stick moves, drag the world to look, tap a slot to select and hold it to dismiss. Attack uses the equipped weapon. Button size and side are adjustable in meditation.

Local practice characters stand in for players. Tap Break Free / B / R3 repeatedly to escape; holding does not count. Creating living creatures and online multiplayer are future work.
